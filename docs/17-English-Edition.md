## Translation conventions

Retain Chinese names and institutions: Lu Zheng (陆峥), Shen Yu’an (沈予安), Su Wan (苏晚), Jiang Chi (江驰), Xu Yan (许砚), Nancheng (南城), An’an (安安市/安市), Nanshi (南市), Jian’an (建安), Jin Xiu Lan Ting. Municipal Public Security Bureau, Criminal Investigation Detachment, procuratorate, local police station, forensic physician, and traffic police retain their Chinese setting; no Western agencies or police ranks are substituted. Team Leader Lu renders 陆队; Dr. Shen renders 沈法医.

Additional names: Old Ghost (老鬼), Zhou Kun (周坤), Lin Wan (林晚), Jiang Xu (江叙), Su Heng (苏蘅), Zheng Mingsheng / Old Zheng (郑明生 / 老郑), Lin Zhinuan (林知暖), Zhou Mingyuan (周明远). La Rose Éternelle retains the French source name. Codenames: the Pruner (修剪匠), White Rose (白玫瑰), Black Rose (黑玫瑰), Iris (鸢尾), Daisy (雏菊), Lily of the Valley (铃兰), Bauhinia (紫荆), Camellia (山茶), Datura (曼陀罗).

Source continuity is preserved, including chapter fifteen’s jump to Old Ghost’s death, shifting dates, member counts, identities/codenames, investigative viewpoints, and fictional forensic claims. No bridging scenes or plot corrections are invented. The source’s 四十斤 is translated as twenty kilos. Fictional dialogue is not medical or procedural guidance.
